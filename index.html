A travel guide / Autumn in Japan

Rachel and Noah’s Japan Honeymoon Itinerary

15 days of city streets, mountain retreats, moss gardens, onsen towns and memorable meals.

01 / The route

Two weeks, five destinations.

Day 01 / Tokyo

Arrival

Morning · Arrive in Tokyo and settle in.

Afternoon · Explore the streets around Shinjuku if energy allows.

Evening · Easy dinner and rest.

View day →
Day 02 / Tokyo

Harajuku to Shibuya

Morning · Meiji Jingu; Harajuku and Omotesando.

Afternoon · Shibuya crossing, shops and cafes. Our top stops: Koffee Mameya for specialty coffee and Iyoshi Cola for craft cola.

Evening · Explore Shinjuku and Golden Gai; we skipped dinner this night.

View day →
Day 03 / Tokyo

Design, coffee & city streets

Morning · Daikanyama T-Site and Ebisu.

Afternoon · Lunch at Pizza Marumo, followed by the Nezu Museum — highly recommended for its art and beautiful garden.

Evening · Dinner in Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane), then drinks at Albatross Bar.

View day →
Day 04 / Tokyo

Shinjuku picnic & Asakusa

Morning · Park Hyatt breakfast buffet, then explore Shinjuku and pick up picnic food at Kinokuniya food hall. Picnic lunch in Shinjuku Gyoen.

Afternoon · Explore Asakusa, visit Senso-ji and graze on street food. Especially recommend Asakusa Menchi for menchi katsu (fried minced-meat cutlets). Stop at Asakusa Batting Stadium.

Evening · Finish the day with massages.

View day →
Day 05 / Hakone

Into the mountains

Morning · Take the Romancecar from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto.

Afternoon · Transfer to Gora; settle into a ryokan.

Evening · Onsen and seasonal kaiseki.

View day →
Day 06 / Hakone / Kyoto

Volcanic landscapes & rail

Morning · Visit Owakudani and try the famous black eggs, said by local legend to add seven years to your life. Walk around Hakone before heading onward.

Afternoon · Travel via Odawara to Kyoto by Shinkansen.

Evening · Check in at Hyatt Place Kyoto, then go record-bar hopping around Pontocho: Fly Me to the Moon, Good Morning Record Bar and Ginza Music Bar — our favorite listening bar ever.

View day →
Day 07 / Kyoto

Arashiyama

Morning · Japanese diner-style breakfast at Takagi Coffee before heading to Arashiyama for the riverside and Togetsukyo Bridge.

Afternoon · Iwatayama Monkey Park; Gio-ji and Jojakko-ji; % Arabica.

Evening · Dinner at Kyoto Taisho for sushi, followed by a walk around Teramachi shopping street.

View day →
Day 08 / Kyoto

Philosopher’s Path

Morning · Ginkaku-ji and a reserved soba lunch at Juugo (十五). Book ahead.

Afternoon · Walk the Philosopher’s Path; explore Nishiki Market for snacks and shopping, plus antique shops.

Evening · Visit teamLab Biovortex Kyoto (reserve ahead), then have gyukatsu (breaded beef cutlet) for dinner.

View day →
Day 09 / Kyoto

Shrines & old streets

Morning · Fushimi Inari at first light; breakfast near the shrine.

Afternoon · Explore Gion and the Kiyomizu-dera area for street food and ceramics. Lunch at Taco Sando — really, really good.

Evening · Izakaya hopping for dinner and cocktails at Bar Ixey — incredible bartending.

View day →
Day 10 / Kinosaki Onsen

A night in an onsen town

Morning · Visit Nijo Castle before the train — highly recommended for history. Pick up ekiben (station bento boxes) for the limited express to Kinosaki Onsen.

Afternoon · Canal walk and ryokan check-in.

Evening · Kaiseki and public bathhouse hopping.

View day →
Day 11 / Osaka

Neon & street food

Morning · Full traditional Japanese breakfast at the ryokan and more public bathhouse hopping, including Ichino-yu, the cave bath. Take the limited express to Osaka.

Afternoon · Settle in; explore Nakanoshima.

Evening · Evening photo shoot in Dotonbori, izakaya dinner and takoyaki, then drinks at Akashic Records, a great cocktail bar.

View day →
Day 12 / Osaka

Shopping & slow living

Morning · Breakfast buffet at Conrad Osaka — we loved it. Start a day of shopping.

Afternoon · Spend most of the day vintage shopping in Shinsaibashi, America-mura and Horie. Optional: Nishikawa custom pillow fitting.

Evening · Hotel downtime and dinner.

View day →
Day 13 / Osaka

Mochi, castle & sake

Morning · Hands-on mochi pounding experience in Osaka.

Afternoon · Visit Osaka Castle — beautiful and great for history.

Evening · Yakiniku dinner, tattoos in America-mura, and a sake tasting at Sake Republic — highly recommended.

View day →
Day 14 / Tokyo

Back to the capital

Morning · Shinkansen from Shin-Osaka to Tokyo.

Afternoon · Return to Tokyo and walk around Ginza.

Evening · Omakase dinner at Sushi Eto — we loved it. Finish with a nightcap at the Conrad Tokyo bar.

View day →
Day 15 / Tokyo

One last morning

Morning · Hamarikyu Gardens or Tsukiji Outer Market.

Afternoon · Ramen for our final lunch in Tokyo.

Evening · Transfer to Haneda with ample time for international departure.

View day →
